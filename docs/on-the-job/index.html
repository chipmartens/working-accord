Before and after, job by job

68 kinds of work, most of them Canadian, before and after AI. In most, the software takes the repetition and a person keeps the call. Six show a job that went away, and what the accord says that person is owed. All are illustrations of how the rules apply, except the four marked Study, which come from published research.

Examples by sector

  1. Study

    Customer support agent

    support desk at one Fortune 500 software company, agents in several countries (study)

    Before

    New agents took months to get good. The answers lived in the heads of a few veterans.

    With AI on the team

    An assistant suggests replies drawn from the best agents' chats. The agent decides what to send. The least-skilled agents gained the most, about 36% more issues solved an hour.1

    Rules 17

  2. Study

    Radiologist, breast screening

    national screening program, Sweden (trial)

    Before

    Two radiologists read every mammogram, thousands a week.

    With AI on the team

    Software sorts the scans and flags spots to look at. Radiologists make every call. In the 2025 analysis of about 106,000 women, they found 29% more cancers with 44% less reading.2

    Rules 28

  3. Illustration

    Line inspector

    auto parts plant, Windsor, Ontario

    Before

    Eight hours under bright lights watching brake rotors roll past, looking for hairline cracks. By the last hour of the shift, every rotor looks the same.

    With AI on the team

    A camera flags anything that looks off. She pulls the flagged rotors and makes the call. When the tool starts rejecting good parts after a lighting change, she catches it.

    Before

    With AI on the team

    A robot cell lays the repeat welds. He sets up each run and signs off on every frame. The hours it frees go to custom repair work and two apprentices, as the shop agrees in writing.

    With AI on the team
