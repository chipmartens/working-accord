Sign the accord

Workers sign to say the ten rules are the floor. Employers sign all ten as a public commitment, or don't sign. Employers are listed with the date on the signers page. Workers' names are never published.

What signing means

As a worker: you're saying these rules are the minimum you expect when AI comes into your workplace. Your name is counted, never shown.

As an employer: you commit to all ten rules, in writing, in public. If a worker reports a breach, we'll ask you to answer and note on the list whether you did.

Read the privacy note before you sign.
