Privacy

If you sign, we use your name and email to confirm and count your signature, and for nothing else. We don't sell it, share it with any company, or add you to a marketing list. Workers' names are never published.

  • What we collect: your name, email, whether you're signing as a worker or employer, your organization (employers only), and optionally your province.
  • What we publish: employers' names and signing dates. Never workers' names.
  • What we don't do: sell or share your details, or send you marketing. You'll get one email to confirm your signature.
  • Removing your name: email marketing@mode40.com and we'll delete your signature and details.
  • No tracking: this site uses no advertising cookies or trackers.

This concept version of the site stores nothing: the form checks what you type and then discards it.
